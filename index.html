Kalpataru Projects International Limited (KPIL) is an Indian engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) company headquartered in Mumbai, founded by Mofatraj Munot.[4][2] the company later diversified into broader infrastructure segments. These include buildings and factories, water supply and irrigation, oil and gas pipelines, and urban mobility: railways, flyovers, metro rail, highways, and airports. 

History[edit | edit source]

The company was incorporated on April 23, 1981 in Gujarat as H.T. Power Structure Private Limited, converted into a public limited company in December 1993, and adopted the name Kalpataru Power Transmission Limited on January 4, 1994.[5][6]

Following an IPO in December 1994, its shares were first listed on Indian stock exchanges in 1995.[6][7]

Following the merger, the company changed its name from Kalpataru Power Transmission Limited to Kalpataru Projects International Limited, effective May 22, 2023.[8][9]

Subsidiaries and acquisitions[edit | edit source]

In March 2019, KPTL acquired an 85% stake in Linjemontage i Grästorp AB, a Swedish EPC company involved in power supply and grid services.[10] In July 2022, its Swedish step-down subsidiary, Kalpataru Power Transmission Sweden AB, acquired the remaining 15% stake for approximately SEK 121 million.[11] Linjemontage was subsequently listed on Nasdaq Stockholm in September 2026.[12] The offering consisted of existing shares with the Kalpataru subsidiary acting as the principal seller, allowing KPIL to retain a majority stake in the company.[13]
